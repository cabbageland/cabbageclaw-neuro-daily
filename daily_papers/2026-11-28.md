## Theme

Measure the missing link: behavior, observability, and control are different achievements.

## Short overview

Two new full-text notes, with different evidential jobs. A ketamine crossover experiment separates increased approach from better reward collection, but faster movement and a fixed-first control visit limit the mechanistic interpretation. A small-animal neural interface records spikes and field potentials across regions, but synchronized acquisition is not causal circuit identification or closed-loop therapy. Pair both with an existing simulation-based active-identification paper to make the next experiment explicit rather than borrowing its conclusion from a title.

## Ranked list

1. **Ketamine promotes strategic and efficient approach-avoidance conflict resolution**
   Directly relevant interventional-psychiatry experiment; adjacent inspiration for computational psychiatry and psychotherapy sequencing. New full-text preserve; Communications Medicine, November 20, 2026.
   Why it matters: In 34 healthy adults, token gains were 57% higher after ketamine than at unmedicated baseline and 33% higher than after lorazepam. Loss sensitivity and movement speed changed too. The randomized active-drug comparison is useful; baseline always came first, and neither plasticity nor CBT synergy was measured. Preserve the behavioral assay, not a claimed antidepressant mechanism.

2. **A Multiscale Neural Interfacing Device for Cross-Brain-Region Electrophysiology**
   Directly relevant methods, neuroengineering, and network measurement. New full-text preserve; Cyborg and Bionic Systems, November 25, 2026.
   Why it matters: A 128-channel-capable acquisition platform combines on-board line-noise filtering and sub-microsecond channel alignment. The simultaneous CA1–NAc experiment uses a 64-channel array in five mice; the coupling test selects one high-quality, active unit per mouse. Useful engineering, but not a validated neural decoder, directed network mechanism, or implemented stimulation loop. The quoted 1.3-g module is not the complete wireless payload.

3. **Beyond the attractor: closed-loop stimulation design for learning multistable neural dynamics**
   Directly relevant computational neuroscience and network/control modeling. Existing preserve; bioRxiv preprint, surfaced November 7. Repository note consulted today, not a fresh primary-source audit.
   Why it matters: PALDI chooses simulated perturbations to learn uncertain dynamics while accounting for hidden-state ambiguity. Its separate control test is the right standard: near-reference prediction still leaves a decision-reversal success gap, 0.76 versus 0.97 for the fitted reference. Real recording hardware does not remove its known-readout and simulated-actuation assumptions.

## Most relevant paper

**Ketamine promotes strategic and efficient approach-avoidance conflict resolution**

The steal-worthy ingredient is the task decomposition: manipulate learned threat probability and explicit loss magnitude separately, record action timing, and compare earned reward rather than equating more approach with improvement. For ketamine plus exposure/CBT studies, this suggests an intermediate behavioral endpoint and a motor-speed control—not evidence that a therapeutic learning window has already been identified.

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

- **Clinical/translational:** Distinguish the randomized ketamine–lorazepam contrast from either drug versus a fixed-first, unmedicated baseline. Add a fully counterbalanced, credible control before attributing the whole gain to drug action.
- **Methods/network measurement:** Report the operating configuration, total wearable mass, noise bandwidth, channel count actually demonstrated, and end-to-end trigger latency. Clock alignment is not controller latency; spike–field coupling is not directed connectivity.
- **Computational/control:** Separate latent-state inference, parameter identification, and held-out intervention success. A proposed bridge would combine synchronized cross-scale recordings with informative perturbations and a separate behavioral endpoint; none of today's sources demonstrates that complete stack.
- **Combination-treatment baseline:** A factorial or sequencing design must test whether ketamine plus psychotherapy adds more than either component and whether task changes mediate durable improvement. The new ketamine experiment contains no psychotherapy arm.

## One-paragraph takeaway

Do not let a useful measurement inherit the status of the mechanism it might eventually test. Higher token yield can partly reflect spending less time exposed to a virtual predator. Cross-regional phase locking can reflect shared state or input. An identified simulator can predict well yet still control imperfectly. The coherent research program is to measure those alternatives, perturb the system, and evaluate a separate meaningful outcome—not collapse acquisition, inference, and benefit into “precision intervention.”
