## Short overview

The new preserve is **Temporal-Targeted Accelerated 40 Hz tACS Enhances Cognitive Function and Network Integration in Alzheimer's Disease: A Randomized Clinical Trial**.

This is the right paper to keep today because it is not just another stimulation-and-score story. The authors randomize clinically diagnosed Alzheimer's disease patients to active or sham bilateral temporal 40 Hz tACS, deliver an intensive four-week course, and measure both cognitive trajectories and resting-state fNIRS connectivity. The active group shows a better ADAS-Cog trajectory, especially total score, memory, and praxis, and the fNIRS analysis identifies a treatment-related frontotemporoparietal connectivity component.

The serious read is narrower than the title. This is useful clinical-network evidence for accelerated temporal gamma-frequency tACS, not proof of disease modification, frequency-specific gamma entrainment, or personalized target engagement. The paper earns preservation because it has the right shape: sham control, explicit dose schedule, network readout, safety reporting, and limitations that matter for the next trial.

## Most relevant paper

**Temporal-Targeted Accelerated 40 Hz tACS Enhances Cognitive Function and Network Integration in Alzheimer's Disease: A Randomized Clinical Trial**

This is the most relevant paper today because it tests a clinically deployable stimulation protocol while also measuring a plausible systems-level effect. Active treatment used 2 mA 40 Hz tACS at T7/T8, 20 minutes per session, twice daily, five days per week, for four weeks. The final analysis included 42 active and 43 sham participants, with assessments at baseline, week 4, and week 16.

The main clinical signal is a significant group-by-time interaction for ADAS-Cog total, plus memory and praxis. The main network signal is one fNIRS component with 38 connections across 18 nodes, involving frontal, temporal, parietal, and sensorimotor regions. Connectivity increased after active stimulation and decreased after sham, and active-group connectivity changes correlated with several cognitive changes.

The caveat is the mechanism. The paper does not include amyloid or tau confirmation, individualized field modeling, direct EEG/MEG gamma entrainment, or causal mediation. So the keep is not "40 Hz tACS solves Alzheimer's." The keep is "a reasonably designed gamma-tACS trial can make the network claim inspectable."

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

- **For AD neuromodulation:** the trial raises the bar because it couples sham-controlled cognitive outcomes to network-level fNIRS change.
- **For gamma-frequency stimulation:** 40 Hz remains a mechanistic hypothesis, not a proven entrainment story here. Without electrophysiology, the frequency-specific claim is incomplete.
- **For targeting:** T7/T8 is practical and repeatable, but not personalized. The existing AD target-modeling note remains the sharper targeting baseline.
- **For network neuroscience:** the fNIRS component is a useful state marker, but it should be treated as cortical hemodynamic accompaniment, not causal proof.
- **For trial design:** accelerated stimulation needs explicit intersession-interval reasoning; this paper at least states the twice-daily schedule and the minimum 4-hour spacing.
- **For hypnosis and hypnotherapy:** today's standing-interest check surfaced mindful hypnotherapy and alcohol-treatment hypnosis RCTs, but neither had the mechanistic or network leverage to beat the tACS preserve.
- **For CBT plus interventional psychiatry:** fresh rTMS-plus-therapy and ketamine-psychotherapy items were useful context, but most were protocols, case reports, or open-label pilots. They stayed below preservation threshold.

## One-paragraph takeaway

September twenty-ninth is a reminder that cognitive neuromodulation should be judged by the whole evidentiary stack: sham control, dose logic, target logic, state readout, clinical durability, and mechanistic humility. The accelerated temporal 40 Hz tACS trial is not definitive, but it is useful because it makes the network claim measurable. The next version should add biomarker-defined AD, individualized electric-field targeting, electrophysiological gamma readouts, and a real mediation test.
